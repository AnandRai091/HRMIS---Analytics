# HR Analytics Dashboard

## Project Overview

This project analyzes employee attrition, workforce demographics, and employee satisfaction to help HR teams identify retention risks and workforce trends.

## Tools Used

* SQL
* Excel
* Power BI

## Business Questions

* What is the overall attrition rate?
* Which department experiences the highest attrition?
* Which age groups are most affected?
* How does education impact attrition?
* What is the employee satisfaction level across job roles?

## Dashboard KPIs

* Total Employees: 1470
* Active Employees: 1233
* Attrition Count: 237
* Attrition Rate: 16.12%
* Average Employee Age: 37

## Key Insights

* R&D accounts for over 56% of total attrition.
* Employees aged 25–34 represent the highest attrition segment.
* Life Sciences employees show the highest attrition among education fields.
* Sales is the second highest contributor to employee turnover.
